Concerts

Regardless of what genres or artists you’re most into, nothing else quite compares with the visceral thrill of seeing great music performed live.

First and foremost, of course, it’s all about the artists themselves. More specifically, it’s about the sheer energy, craft and intensity they bring to the stage, whether they’re playing to 30 people or 30,000. There’s a reason we tend to think of tracks played live as ‘music in its purest form’.

How many times have we all searched for online clips of our favorite songs or performers, and then tacked the word ‘live’ on to the end of the song title? When we do, we know there’s a good chance we’ll dig up something that hits harder, sounds fresher, or feels more emotionally impactful than even the most iconic studio recordings can hope to achieve.

On top of those uniquely direct and unfiltered stage performances, there are the countless other intangibles that help contribute to a full concert experience: the venue, the sound system, the sheer electricity of an audience caught up in the moment.

That intense feeling of community, camaraderie and shared passion.

The sense of being present at a real one-off occasion, whether that’s in the flesh or broadcast live online, witnessed only by those in the know. Those who love the same things you do. Your people - your tribe, even. That’s the strength of feeling that truly great concerts can convey.

Here at Songkick, it’s exactly the way we’ve always felt too. And it’s precisely why, since 2007, we’ve been dedicated to making it as easy, fun and fair as possible for you to experience the magic for yourselves.

Getting concert tickets

Let’s talk about how we do it.

If you’ve ever tried to buy concert tickets for an especially hyped show, you’ll already know how important it is to be ahead of the game. Especially so if you’ve already experienced the bitter disappointment of missing out - and, let’s face it, that’s happened to a lot of us at one time or another!

On the one hand, it’s hardly surprising: after all, many of the most iconic live performances end up being looked back upon as nothing less than cultural watershed moments. They can be the spark that ignites a career, kickstarts a whole scene, or even defines an entire musical epoch.

On the other hand, none of us much like the idea of being the one who wasn’t there, and the industry statistics back this up pretty convincingly. In fact, the recent Nielsen Music 360 Report shows that more than half (52%) of all Americans attended a live music event of some kind during 2018-19.

As a hopeful attendee, it’s often tricky to tell which concerts will see the highest demand-to-availability ratios. Sometimes, those intimate club gigs with strictly limited capacity can seem the hardest to jump on quickly enough. Other times, it’s just as difficult to get in line for a huge arena show by an iconic act that rarely tours near you.

Either way, your aim is much the same: you just want to make sure you’re part of the in-crowd whenever the next concert by a favorite act rolls around, right? And that’s where Songkick comes in.

That’s why we’ve built a suite of easy, responsive, and absolutely free smart apps and searchable web tools, giving you the power to track your own top artists, bands, shows and tours.
